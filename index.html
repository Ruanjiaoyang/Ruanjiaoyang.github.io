About关于我

Blog博客

DDPM: Add Noise Step by Step, Then Learn to Remove ItDDPM：一步步加噪，再一步步去噪

DDPM defines a fixed Markov chain that gradually turns data into Gaussian noise and trains a network to reverse it. This post derives, step by step, the closed-form marginal q(x_t|x_0), the tractable posterior q(x_{t-1}|x_t,x_0) by completing the square, the full variational bound and its decomposition into L_T, L_{t-1} and L_0, and how the Gaussian KL terms collapse to the simple noise-prediction loss. It then covers the training and sampling algorithms, the choice of reverse variance, noise schedules, network architecture, and the epsilon, x_0 and v parameterizations.

Date: October 16, 2026 | Estimated Reading Time: 26 min | Author: Jiaoyang Ruan

From AE to VAE to VQ-VAE: Making the Latent Space Samplable从 AE 到 VAE 再到 VQ-VAE：让隐空间可以采样

An autoencoder compresses data but cannot generate, because nothing constrains its latent space. VAEs fix this with a probabilistic encoder and a KL regularizer. This post derives the evidence lower bound in two ways, the closed-form Gaussian KL and the reparameterization trick, explains blurriness and posterior collapse, and then covers VQ-VAE in detail: codebook, the three loss terms, the straight-through estimator, EMA updates, the learned prior, codebook collapse, VQ-VAE-2 and VQGAN, which together became the tokenizer behind latent diffusion and autoregressive image models.

Date: October 16, 2026 | Estimated Reading Time: 25 min | Author: Jiaoyang Ruan

GAN: Learning a Distribution Through a Two-Player GameGAN：用一场博弈学习数据分布

GANs learn to sample from a data distribution without ever writing down its density. This post covers the generator and discriminator, derives the minimax objective, the optimal discriminator and the Jensen–Shannon connection, explains the non-saturating loss, walks through architecture and training, and analyzes vanishing gradients, mode collapse and unstable dynamics, followed by the main extensions (DCGAN, WGAN/WGAN-GP, LSGAN, SNGAN, CGAN, Pix2Pix, CycleGAN, StyleGAN, BigGAN) and applications.

Date: October 16, 2026 | Estimated Reading Time: 23 min | Author: Jiaoyang Ruan
